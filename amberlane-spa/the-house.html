18 Willow Lane

A townhouse that learned to be quiet.

Rooms, courtyard, water

Amberlane occupies a restored townhouse on Willow Lane. Eight treatment rooms, a steam, a sauna, and a still-water plunge in the courtyard. The front desk takes your phone. Tea is poured in the sitting room, not from a machine in a corridor.

  • Ground floor: reception, sitting room, thermal circuit
  • First floor: four massage rooms with ensuite rinse
  • Second floor: two facial rooms and two wrap rooms
  • Courtyard: plunge and rest beds, weather permitting
