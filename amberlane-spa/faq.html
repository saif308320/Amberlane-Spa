Before you visit

The questions we answer most.

How do I hold a time?

Send a request on the book page. We confirm by email within a few hours on open days. Same-day is sometimes possible after 14:00 cancellations — call the desk.

Cancellation?

24 hours. Inside that window the ritual is charged unless we fill the room. Members: 12 hours.

Gratuity?

Not required. If you wish, the desk can add it. Therapists do not handle cash in the room.

Should I eat first?

A light meal is fine. Avoid arriving hungry for wraps and the circuit — heat is harder on an empty stomach.

Health notes?

Tell us about pregnancy, recent surgery, blood pressure, and skin conditions on the form. We will adapt or decline kindly if a ritual is wrong that day.

Scent?

Say unscented. We mean it. Shared spaces are already kept quiet of fragrance.

Phones?

They stay at the desk. If you are on call, tell us — we will keep the device at reception and knock only if it cannot wait.

Parking?

Two house spaces behind the lane, first come. Otherwise street. A map arrives with confirmation.

Accessibility?

Ground-floor treatment is available with notice. The thermal circuit has steps. Write us before you book.
